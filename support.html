Support

Updated: 20 September 2026

Contact us

Write to dmkstudio.dev@gmail.com. We answer in English and usually reply within two business days.

To help us find the problem quickly, please include your device model, the operating system version, the level number you were on, and a screenshot if you have one.

Common questions

I paid, but the purchase did not arrive

Open Shop in the game and tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the coin shelf. If the item is still missing, write to us and attach the receipt from the App Store or Google Play.

My progress disappeared

Progress is stored on the device. Reinstalling the game, resetting the device, or clearing app data removes it. If progress vanished without any of those, please write to us.

How do I change my ad choices?

Open Settings and find Personalised ads under Privacy. The switch turns personalised advertising on or off at any time.

How do I remove ads?

The No Ads item in the in-game shop removes interstitial ads permanently. Rewarded videos stay available, because they are the ones that pay you coins.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the game removes local data. For the online leaderboard, write to us and we will remove your entry and nickname.
